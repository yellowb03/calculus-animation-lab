# Calculus Animation Lab

A local, offline study app for a calculus student preparing with assigned textbook homework. Teach the algebraic operations and the geometry together through live animation. Success is the student controlling each transformation and seeing why it is valid, rather than watching a prerecorded result.

User commitments: pure black stage; one white LaTeX equation at a time; active changes in cyan/red; concise explanation to the right; animated Cartesian curves/2D objects; Back/Next and interactive timing; reusable for new problems without video rendering. Initial content:12 selected assigned exercises spanning HW2-HW7. New expression generation is bounded to reliable supported methods, with clear unsupported errors. Generic authored step import extends the same renderer to other problems. No claims that selected exercises are on the actual exam.
